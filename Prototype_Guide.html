The Northstar Pay example

Read this short case before or after exploring the prototype. No architecture document is needed to understand the example.

The business question

Northstar Pay is a fictional payment company in France. It wants to prepare evidence for a card security audit and a digital resilience audit. The source views are illustrative examples labelled PCI DSS (Payment Card Industry Data Security Standard) and DORA (Digital Operational Resilience Act). Both invented statements address privileged access. The resilience illustration has an additional duty: record why certain access remains in place. The product must help the company prepare the shared part once while keeping that extra duty and each audit judgement distinct.

Who does what?

  • Maya, company compliance lead: declares which payment and merchant services are in scope and chooses when company preparation adopts an approved catalogue update.
  • Clara, control owner: supplies an access-review file for April–June 2026 and describes its coverage. One merchant service is still missing from the observed inventory.
  • Catalogue curator and qualified expert: prepare and review a proposed shared requirement. The expert decides the exact meaning; software may publish it only while approval and source-use rights remain valid.
  • Alex, lead auditor: independently judges whether a particular evidence version is relevant for a specific audit, handles findings and authorises an exact report and audience.

What the demonstration does and does not do

The pages and navigation work offline in a browser. The displayed records are fixed examples. Explanation buttons describe intended behaviour; they do not upload evidence, run an AI model, approve a requirement, publish a catalogue release or issue a report. A complete target contains more than this story: explore all modules for the additional illustrative screens.

This case intentionally invents its PCI DSS and DORA requirement summaries and their relationship. It does not assert that any particular real clause is equivalent, applicable to a specific entity, or licensed for redistribution. Product, audit and legal specialists must qualify real content separately.
